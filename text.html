Message from the COO

Vinu Chakravarthy Chief Operating Officer, University of Aberdeen Mumbai

It is my pleasure to welcome you to the University of Aberdeen Mumbai Placement Profile Book 2026–27. As we establish the University of Aberdeen's presence in India, we bring to Mumbai the strength of a university with over 530 years of academic excellence, innovation and global impact.

Our postgraduate programmes are designed to combine strong disciplinary knowledge with the skills, adaptability and global perspective required in a rapidly changing world. Industry engagement is central to this vision, and we are committed to preparing graduates who can contribute meaningfully to organisations from the outset.

As we build the University of Aberdeen Mumbai, we invite industry partners to engage with our students through recruitment, projects, mentoring and other opportunities. We look forward to building enduring partnerships that create value for industry, our students and the wider community.

As we welcome our founding postgraduate cohort at the University of Aberdeen Mumbai, our focus is on preparing students to apply their academic learning, build strong professional capabilities and contribute effectively in the workplace.

Career readiness is an integral part of the student journey at the University of Aberdeen Mumbai. Through individual career preparation, industry interactions, mentoring and employer engagement, we are working closely with our students to help them understand their strengths, sharpen their aspirations and prepare for opportunities that align with their capabilities.

This Placement Profile Book introduces our MBA and MSc Artificial Intelligence students and the diverse experiences, skills and perspectives they bring. We look forward to engaging with organisations not only through recruitment, but also through projects, mentoring, industry collaboration and longer-term partnerships.
